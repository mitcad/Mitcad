# Swept handle

Model a drawer pull handle: a round bar 10 mm in diameter swept along a path that rises 30 mm straight up from the drawer front at each end, joined by a straight grip 96 mm between the centres of the two legs, with 12 mm radius bends between the legs and the grip. Each leg ends in a 16 mm diameter, 4 mm thick foot on the drawer front with an M4 tapped hole 8 mm deep along the leg's axis. The hole spacing will change later.
