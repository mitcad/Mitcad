# Two component assembly

Model a small assembly of two components: a base block 50 mm x 30 mm x 15 mm with a 10 mm diameter through hole in its middle along its height, and a pin 10 mm in diameter and 40 mm long with a 16 mm diameter, 3 mm thick head. Make each a component of its own and put the pin into the hole, its head resting on the top of the block, so that it can still turn about its axis. The pin's diameter will change later, and the hole has to follow it.
