# Shelled housing

Model the lower half of a small electronics housing: an open-topped box 90 mm x 60 mm outside and 30 mm high, with 2 mm walls and floor and 4 mm rounded vertical outside edges. Inside, put a screw boss in each corner: 7 mm outside diameter, reaching up to 2 mm below the rim, with a 2.5 mm pilot hole for a self-tapping screw. The housing will be made in other sizes later.
