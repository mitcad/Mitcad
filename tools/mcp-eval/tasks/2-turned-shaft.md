# Turned shaft

Model a turned steel shaft: 120 mm long, 20 mm in diameter, with a 15 mm diameter journal 25 mm long at each end. Cut a retaining ring groove 1.1 mm wide and 13.8 mm in diameter in each journal, 2 mm from the shoulder. Chamfer both ends 1 mm x 45°. The shaft will be made in other lengths later.
