# Printed clip

Model a wall clip for a 22 mm tube, to be 3D printed lying on its back plate. The back plate is 40 mm x 30 mm x 4 mm with one counterbored hole for a 4 mm screw (counterbore 8 mm diameter, 2.5 mm deep) in its middle. The clip ring stands on the plate with 2.5 mm walls, holds the tube from the side through an opening 18 mm wide, and its axis runs horizontally, parallel to the plate. Keep the model printable without supports on the plate: no horizontal overhangs wider than a few millimetres and a horizontal hole shaped so that it prints. The tube size will change later.
