# Plate bracket

Model an L-shaped mounting bracket bent from 3 mm steel plate. The base leg is 60 mm long and the upright leg 45 mm high, both 40 mm wide, with a 3 mm inside bend radius. The base leg has two 6.5 mm holes 25 mm apart, centred across the width and 20 mm from the free end; the upright leg has one 8.5 mm hole centred 30 mm above the base. The bracket will be made in other widths later.
